Product Requirements Document (PRD): proSIM

Version: 3.0 (Pedagogy Aligned)
Status: Active Governance

1. Product Vision

To provide the most realistic, high-fidelity "Flight Simulator" for B2B Product Managers, prioritizing systems thinking and trade-off management over gamification.

2. Strategic Pillars (Derived from PEDAGOGY.md)

Pillar A: The Iron Quadrant (Physics)

Requirement: The engine must enforce zero-sum trade-offs between Health, Trust, Morale, and Score.

Constraint: No decision can improve all four metrics simultaneously.

Pillar B: The Learning Operating System (Pedagogy)

Requirement: The UX must enforce the "Prediction Loop."

Requirement: The Analytics Engine must support "Progressive Disclosure" based on player level.

Requirement: Content must be hyperlinked to the Knowledge Graph (Wiki).

Pillar C: Data Verisimilitude (Realism)

Requirement: Analytics must mirror industry standards (Mixpanel/Amplitude).

Requirement: Narratives must be data-driven (Leading Indicators in charts precede Narrative Triggers in Inbox).

4. Success Metrics

Retention: Students complete Level 1 without "Rage Quitting" (Adaptive Friction).

Competence: Students' "Product Sense Score" increases between Q1 and Q2.

Portability: 100% of finished sessions result in an exported Artifact (Certificate/Report).
