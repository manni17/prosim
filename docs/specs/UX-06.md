# UX-06: Progressive Tab Unlock

## Requirements
- **Goal:** Manage cognitive load by locking advanced analytics tabs until the player reaches the appropriate level.
- **Locking Logic:**
  - **Level 1:** Only 'Overview' and 'Insights' are available.
  - **Level 2:** 'Funnels' and 'Retention' are unlocked.
  - **Level 3:** 'Flows' and 'Wiki/Dictionary' (if applicable) are unlocked.
- **UI Feedback:**
  - Locked tabs display a Padlock icon.
  - Text is desaturated/grayed out.
  - Tooltip or Toast appears on click: "Security Clearance Required (Level X)".

## DoD
- Tabs are visually distinct when locked.
- Interaction is disabled for locked tabs.
- Reaching Level 2 dynamically unlocks the 'Funnels' and 'Retention' tabs.
