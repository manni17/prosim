Pedagogical Governance (Source of Truth)

Philosophy: Competence through Consequence.
Goal: Move the student from "Passive Observer" to "Active Strategist" via high-stakes simulation.

1. Core Learning Mechanics

A. The Prediction Loop (Metacognition)

Principle: Learning happens when a prediction is falsified.

Implementation: Critical strategic turns (Turn 3, 12) MUST intercept the user and force a "Hypothesis Lock" (predicting Metric Delta) before execution.

Success Metric: "Product Sense Score" (Correlation between Prediction and Reality).

B. Scaffolded Autonomy (Cognitive Load Management)

Principle: Novices drown in data; experts swim in it.

Implementation:

Level 1: Locking advanced tools (Retention/Flows Tabs).

Level 2: Unlock tools only when the student encounters the specific problem they solve (e.g., Churn crisis unlocks Retention Tab).

C. Just-in-Time Knowledge (Contextual Learning)

Principle: Definitions are useless without context.

Implementation: "Blue Text" keywords in Narrative Content link directly to the Wiki. Users learn definitions while solving the problem.

D. Adaptive Friction (Flow State)

Principle: Failure must be instructive, not terminal.

Implementation:

Repeated Failure: If learning_profile["failures"] > 3, the RNG generator reduces crisis_probability by 20% to allow recovery.

Mastery: If score > S-Rank, introduce "Black Swan" events to force improvisation.

## 3. The Competency Scorecard (Measurement)

We do not grade on "Did you win?" We grade on "How did you think?"

### A. Conviction Score (Confidence)
* **Signal:** `Time_to_Decide` + `Revisited_Decision_Count`.
* **Logic:** Fast decisions with low hovering = High Conviction.
* **Pedagogy:** Leaders must act with incomplete information.

### B. Strategic Consistency (Focus)
* **Signal:** `Metadata.Is_Reversal` + `Alignment_Score`.
* **Logic:** If a user chooses "Project Floodgate" (Growth) but then cuts the Marketing Budget, Consistency drops.
* **Pedagogy:** Strategy is the refusal to do things that don't fit the goal.

### C. Cognitive Durability (Resilience)
* **Signal:** `Performance_Delta` after a "Crisis Event" (e.g., Server Outage).
* **Logic:** Does the user's error rate increase immediately after a negative narrative trigger?
* **Pedagogy:** Measuring grace under pressure.
